# two42

Software for the people in a church's groups: who's in them, what's coming up, who's serving, and what to pray for. These are the words the product uses with its people — copy, docs, and tickets use them and avoid the alternatives listed.

## Language

### Places and people

**Church**:
The community that runs a two42 space and contains its groups. In copy, use the church's own name; fall back to "your church" when the name isn't known.
_Avoid_: organization, org, tenant (outside the platform operator's screens and engineering docs)

**Group**:
A recurring gathering of people within a church — a Sunday school class, connection group, small group, or anything similar. The church names each group; the product always calls the kind of thing a "group".
_Avoid_: class, small group, ministry, team

**Member**:
A person who belongs to a group.
_Avoid_: user, person (in copy), attendee

**Household**:
The people who live together and are listed together in the directory. A surname may label one ("The Okafor family"), but the thing is always a household.
_Avoid_: family (as the name of the thing)

### Roles

**Leader**:
A member who runs a group: approves requests to join it, publishes its content, and manages its teams. Say "group leader" only where "leader" alone is ambiguous. A group can have several leaders, each with the same rights; "co-leader" describes one of them, not a separate role.
_Avoid_: group admin, organizer, host, teacher

**Church admin**:
A person who manages the church's whole two42 space — its people, households, groups, and settings.
_Avoid_: org admin, admin (alone, in member-facing copy), group admin

**Team lead**:
A member who leads one serving team within a group.
_Avoid_: team leader, captain

**Pending**:
A person who has signed in but whom the church has not approved yet. They see only their own profile.
_Avoid_: unapproved, guest

**Operator**:
A person on the two42 team who sets up churches and keeps the platform running. Being an operator grants nothing inside a church.
_Avoid_: platform admin, superuser (in copy)

### Life of a group

**Event**:
Anything on a group's calendar, including the group's own regular meetings. The calendar is where events appear, never the thing itself.
_Avoid_: meeting, gathering, calendar item, session, occurrence (in copy)

**Team**:
A set of members within a group who take turns serving at its events.
_Avoid_: ministry, serving role, group

**Serving**:
Taking a turn on a team for a specific event date.
_Avoid_: volunteering, signup, shift

**Lesson**:
A teaching given to a group, with its notes and any recording. Lessons may belong to a series.
_Avoid_: lecture, class, sermon, talk

**Series**:
An ordered set of lessons under one title and teacher.
_Avoid_: course, unit

**Teacher**:
The person who gives a lesson or series.
_Avoid_: speaker, lecturer

**Fund**:
A named way for members to give money to a person or cause in the group. A fund goes to someone, named on the fund as "Goes to {name}".
_Avoid_: collection, donation page; steward and co-collector (for the person it goes to)

### Actions

**Add**:
Put something new into an existing list ("Add event", "Add fund").
_Avoid_: Create, New, "+ Add"

**Delete**:
Destroy something permanently.
_Avoid_: Remove (for things), Archive

**Remove**:
Take a person out of a group, team, or household. The person still exists.
_Avoid_: Delete (for people), Kick

**Revoke**:
Make an invite link stop working.

**Retire**:
Stop a fund from taking gifts while keeping its history.
_Avoid_: Archive, Close

**Decline**:
Turn down a request to join.
_Avoid_: Deny, Reject

**Sign in** / **Sign out**:
Start or end a session.
_Avoid_: Log in, Login, Log out
