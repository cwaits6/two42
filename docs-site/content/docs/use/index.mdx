## What two42 is

two42 is where the people in your church's groups see what's coming up, who's serving, what to pray for, and how to reach each other. Your church runs its own two42 space, and everything you see belongs to that church.

A **group** is a recurring gathering in your church: a Sunday school class, a connection group, or anything similar. Your church names each one. You can belong to more than one group, and each group has its own calendar, announcements, prayer requests, lessons, serving teams and funds.

## Who does what

- A **member** belongs to a group and sees its pages.
- A **leader** runs a group: adds events and announcements, approves requests to join, and manages its teams. A group can have several leaders with the same rights.
- A **team lead** runs one serving team within a group.
- A **church admin** manages the church's whole space: its people, households, groups and settings. Being a church admin doesn't by itself show you a group's content; you see it by joining the group.

What leaders and church admins can do is covered in [Roles](/docs/administer/roles).

## Find your way around

On a computer, the menu is the sidebar on the left. On a phone, select **Menu** at the top right to open it. The wordmark at the top always takes you to **Home**.

### Inside a group

The group's name sits at the top of the menu. Below it are the group's pages:

1. **Home**, **Calendar**, **Announcements**
2. **Prayer**, **Lessons**, **Serving**, **Dinner club**
3. **Directory**, **Give**, **About**

**Serving** shows only when you're on a team that has signing up turned on. **Dinner club** shows only when a leader of the group turns it on; see [Dinner club](/docs/use/dinner-club). Leaders also see **Group settings** below a line, with the number of people waiting to join, if any.

### When you're in more than one group

The group name at the top of the menu becomes a switcher. Select it to move to another group; you stay on the same kind of page. Its first row, **All groups**, opens a Home that covers all your groups, where the menu holds **Home**, **Calendar** and **Directory**.

### Find a group

When your church lets members find groups, **Find a group** appears in the menu: as the last row of the group switcher, or as a row below the group's pages if you're in one group. See [Joining a group and a household](/docs/use/joining).

### Your account

Your photo or initials and your name sit at the bottom of the menu. Select them to open the account menu:

- **My profile** and **Settings**
- **Church admin**, for church admins only
- **Help**, **Privacy** and **Terms**
- **Sign out**
