## What a fund is

A fund is a named way to give money to a person or cause in your group. Each fund goes to one member of the group, shown on the fund as **Goes to** their name, sometimes with a second person beside them and a role such as Treasurer. A name shows with its suffix, such as Jr., when the person has added one to their profile.

two42 doesn't handle the money. Each payment button opens the payment app or copies the account, and you send the gift there. two42 doesn't record whether you gave or how much.

## Give to a fund

1. Choose **Give** in the menu.
2. Select a fund to open it. The first fund in the list starts open.
3. Pick a payment method and follow its button.

A fund with a last day shows a **Through** tag with the date. After that date it no longer appears on Give.

## How each payment method works

| Method | What the button does |
|--------|----------------------|
| Venmo, on a phone or tablet | Enter an **Amount (optional)**, then select **Open Venmo**. Venmo opens with a payment to the account, the fund's name as the note, and the amount if you entered one. |
| Venmo, on a computer | Select **Copy** to copy the Venmo account, or scan the QR code with your phone's camera to pay in Venmo. |
| PayPal | **Open PayPal** opens the account's PayPal page in a new tab. |
| Cash App | **Open Cash App** opens the account's Cash App page in a new tab. |
| Zelle | **Copy** copies the email or phone number. Paste it into Zelle to send. |
| Apple / Google Pay | **Copy** copies the phone number. Paste it into your payment app to send. |

The amount you type for Venmo is only passed to Venmo. two42 never saves it.

## Who can add funds

Leaders of the group can add, edit, retire and delete any fund in it.

A church admin can let members add their own funds with the **Members can add their own funds** setting in **Church settings**. When it's on, any member can add a fund that goes to themself, and edit their own funds. When it's off, only leaders can add and edit funds. Funds that already exist stay on Give either way. See [Church admin pages](/docs/administer/church-admin).

## Add a fund

1. On **Give**, select **Add fund**.
2. Enter a **Fund name** and, if you like, a **Short description**.
3. Under **Who receives it?**, choose the member whose accounts the money goes to. Leaders can pick anyone in the group; a member adding their own fund is the recipient.
4. Optionally choose a **Second person** to name beside them, such as a spouse, and **Their role**, shown after their name.
5. Optionally set **Retire on**. The fund is hidden from Give after that date.
6. Under **Payment methods**, turn on at least one method and enter its account, for example a Venmo handle, a PayPal.me name, a Cash App $cashtag, or the email or phone number for Zelle.
7. Check **How members see it**, then select **Add fund**.

The recipient and the second person must both be members of the group.

## Edit, retire or delete a fund

Open the fund on Give and select **Edit fund**. On the edit page:

- Change any field and select **Save changes**.
- Select **Retire fund** to stop it taking gifts while keeping it. Select **Reopen fund** to put it back.
- Select **Delete fund**, then confirm, to remove the fund and its payment methods for good.

## Funds hidden from members

Leaders see a **Hidden from members** section at the bottom of Give. It lists funds members can't give to, each with its status, and an **Edit** or **Review** button:

- **Retired**: a leader retired it, or its **Retire on** date has passed.
- **Needs a recipient**: the person it went to left the group and there was no second person to take over.
- **Needs review**: the person it went to left the group and the second person now receives it.

When members can add their own funds, the person a fund now goes to also sees it here while it needs review, so they can correct its payment accounts.

A fund with no payment account doesn't appear to members at all. Leaders still see it in the list, with a note that members can't give to it until it has a payment method.

## Confirm a fund after its recipient leaves

When the person a fund goes to leaves the group or deletes their account, the fund isn't deleted. If a second person is named and still in the group, the fund passes to them; otherwise nobody receives it. Either way it stops taking gifts, because its payment accounts may still pay the person who left.

To put it back on Give, a leader of the group:

1. Selects **Review** on the fund under **Hidden from members**.
2. Chooses who receives it, if nobody does.
3. Checks each payment account and corrects any that still pay the person who left.
4. Selects **Confirm fund**, then confirms.

Only a leader can confirm a fund. A fund that was retired stays retired after it's confirmed, until a leader reopens it.
