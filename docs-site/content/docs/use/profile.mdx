## Open your profile

Open the account menu at the bottom of the menu, where your photo and name are, and choose **My profile**.

If you haven't finished setting up your account, two42 takes you to setup first. See [Sign in](/docs/use/sign-in).

When you're in a household, the page has two tabs: **Me** for your own details, and **Household** for the household you share. Without a household, the page shows your own details only.

## See how others see you

The top of the **Me** tab, **How others see you**, shows your photo, name, mobile phone and city as they appear in the directory. If you're hidden from the directory, it says so instead.

Select **Preview my directory entry** to see your full directory card, with your email setting applied, the way other members see it.

## Change your photo

1. Select **Change photo** and pick an image.
2. In **Crop photo**, drag the photo to move it, and pinch or use the **Zoom** slider to zoom. With a keyboard, the arrow keys move it and plus and minus zoom.
3. Select **Save photo**.

The new photo is saved straight away; you don't need to select **Save changes**. Your photo shows in the directory, on prayer requests you post with your name, and in the menu.

## Edit your details

Everything you add on this page shows in the directory, except a birth year, which is only included when someone saves you as a phone contact. Title and gender aren't shown there. Leave out anything you don't want shown.

You can edit:

- **First name**, **Last name** and **Preferred name**. If your last name differs from your household's, turn on **My last name is different from my household's** to enter your own.
- **Title** (such as Dr. or Pastor) and **Suffix** (such as Jr. or III). A suffix shows after your name everywhere your name shows. A title shows only on your profile and on a group's **Leadership** list on its About page.
- **Mobile phone**, **Home phone** and **Work phone**.
- **Birthday**: a month and day, with an optional year.
- Your address: **Street address**, **Apt / unit**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP**. If your household has an address, these show only when you turn on **My address is different from my household's**; otherwise the household's address is used.
- **Gender**: **Male** or **Female**. It lets people filter the directory by Male or Female, and isn't shown on your entry. Select the chosen one again to clear it.
- **Occupation** and **Employer**, so people in your groups can find help from someone they know.

Select **Save changes** when you're done.

## Details from your church's records

If your church keeps its records in church software and has connected it, some of your details may be filled in from those records. The first time that happens, the top of **My profile** says **Your church filled in some of your details from its records.** and links to each filled-in detail you can change. Select **Got it** and it doesn't show again.

Under each detail that came from your church:

- **From your church's records** means two42 keeps it up to date when your church's office changes it.
- **Managed by your church** means your church has locked it. It shows as text, with nothing to edit. Only your name, suffix, title, gender and a child's grade can be locked.
- **Changed in two42** means you changed or cleared it, and it's yours from then on: updates from your church no longer replace it. Select **Use your church's value** to go back to what your church has.

The same labels show on the **Household** tab: under the address, the home phone and the anniversary, which are the household's own, and on the edit sheet for a spouse or anyone else in your household whose profile you edit. A locked detail shows as text there too, and when you set up your profile.

Your phone numbers, birthday, anniversary, photo and your household's address and home phone are always yours to change or clear, and a detail you clear stays empty. Either the primary or the spouse can change or clear the household's. Your preferred name is filled in from your church only while you haven't set one. Your sign-in email is never changed. A photo from your church is stored in two42; select **Remove photo** to take it off, or upload your own, which your church's photo never replaces.

### Sharing your details with your church's office

If your church has asked for it, setting up your account and your profile shows a box, **Share my details with** your church's name **'s office**, unticked. Ticking it sends your first and last name, sign-in email and phone to the church office once, so they can add you to the church's records. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent if you leave it unticked or you're already linked. Once the office adds you, a church admin can link you to your record like anyone else. See [Set up your profile](/docs/use/sign-in#set-up-your-profile).

## Change your email

Your email shows on the profile but can't be edited there. Change it in [Settings](/docs/use/settings#change-your-email).

## Hide details from the directory

- Turn on **Hide my email from the directory** to keep your email off your directory entry.
- Turn on **Hide me from the directory** to leave the directory altogether.

Select **Save changes** after either one. What each does, and what happens to your household, is in [Directory](/docs/use/directory).

## Your household

The **Household** tab shows:

- **Household details**: the household's name, address, phone and anniversary.
- **Members with an account**: you and the other people in the household who sign in. Select **Edit** beside your own name to go back to the **Me** tab.
- **Others in your household**: children and anyone else without their own account.

Only people listed in the household as **Primary** or **Spouse** can change it. They can:

- Change the household details and select **Save household**.
- Edit the other members with an account.
- Select **Add a member** to link someone who already has an account.
- Select **Add someone without an account** to list a child or another relative, and edit or remove the people listed there.

### Children suggested from your church's records

If your church has turned on suggesting children, the **Primary** and **Spouse** see **Suggested from your church's records** on the **Household** tab, listing the children your church lists in your family, with their birthday (never the year) and grade. When your church shows details live from its records, a suggestion shows the child's name only, and once added their birthday and grade show live in the directory until you set them. For each child:

- **Add to household** lists them under **Others in your household**, with no birth year. A photo from your church comes a day or so later; it never replaces one you upload. If someone in your household already has the child's name, that entry is kept instead of a second one.
- **Dismiss** asks first: two42 won't suggest them again. You can still add them yourself.

Nobody else sees these suggestions, and nobody sees them while your church has suggesting children turned off.

For a child, the form has a **Grade**: **No grade**, **Kindergarten**, or **1st grade** through **12th grade**. The grade moves up on its own each August, so you set it once. It shows beside the child, as "3rd grade", in **Others in your household** and on the household's directory card.

Everyone else in the household sees these details and edits only their own profile.

How households join and appear in the directory is in [Joining a group and a household](/docs/use/joining) and [Directory](/docs/use/directory).
