## Open your profile

Open the account menu at the bottom of the menu, where your photo and name are, and choose **My profile**.

If you haven't finished setting up your account, two42 takes you to setup first. See [Sign in](/docs/use/sign-in).

When you're in a household, the page has two tabs: **Me** for your own details, and **Household** for the household you share. Without a household, the page shows your own details only.

## See how others see you

The top of the **Me** tab, **How others see you**, shows your photo, name, mobile phone and city as they appear in the directory. If you're hidden from the directory, it says so instead.

Select **Preview my directory entry** to see your full directory card, with your email setting applied, the way other members see it.

## Change your photo

1. Select **Change photo** and pick an image.
2. In **Crop photo**, drag the photo to move it, and pinch or use the **Zoom** slider to zoom. With a keyboard, the arrow keys move it and plus and minus zoom.
3. Select **Save photo**.

The new photo is saved straight away; you don't need to select **Save changes**. Your photo shows in the directory, on prayer requests you post with your name, and in the menu.

## Edit your details

Everything you add on this page shows in the directory, except a birth year, which is only included when someone saves you as a phone contact. Leave out anything you don't want shown.

You can edit:

- **First name**, **Last name** and **Preferred name**. If your last name differs from your household's, turn on **My last name is different from my household's** to enter your own.
- **Mobile phone**, **Home phone** and **Work phone**.
- **Birthday**: a month and day, with an optional year.
- Your address: **Street address**, **Apt / unit**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP**. If your household has an address, these show only when you turn on **My address is different from my household's**; otherwise the household's address is used.
- **Occupation** and **Employer**, so people in your groups can find help from someone they know.

Select **Save changes** when you're done.

## Change your email

Your email shows on the profile but can't be edited there. Change it in [Settings](/docs/use/settings#change-your-email).

## Hide details from the directory

- Turn on **Hide my email from the directory** to keep your email off your directory entry.
- Turn on **Hide me from the directory** to leave the directory altogether.

Select **Save changes** after either one. What each does, and what happens to your household, is in [Directory](/docs/use/directory).

## Your household

The **Household** tab shows:

- **Household details**: the household's name, address, phone and anniversary.
- **Members with an account**: you and the other people in the household who sign in. Select **Edit** beside your own name to go back to the **Me** tab.
- **Others in your household**: children and anyone else without their own account.

Only people listed in the household as **Primary** or **Spouse** can change it. They can:

- Change the household details and select **Save household**.
- Edit the other members with an account.
- Select **Add a member** to link someone who already has an account.
- Select **Add someone without an account** to list a child or another relative, and edit or remove the people listed there.

Everyone else in the household sees these details and edits only their own profile.

How households join and appear in the directory is in [Joining a group and a household](/docs/use/joining) and [Directory](/docs/use/directory).
