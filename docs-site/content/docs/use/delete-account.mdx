## Before you start

Deleting your account can't be undone. If you want a copy of your data, [download it](/docs/use/settings#download-your-data) first.

You can't delete your account yourself in two cases:

- **You're the only church admin.** A church needs at least one church admin, so make someone else a church admin first.
- **You're a platform operator.** Your operator access has to be removed first.

If a church admin hasn't approved you yet, Settings isn't open to you. Ask a church admin to delete your account.

## Delete your account

1. Open the account menu and choose **Settings**.
2. Under **Your data**, select **Delete my account**.
3. Read the confirmation. Under **What goes** and **What stays** it names the household, funds and groups your account affects.
4. Select **Delete my account** to confirm.

You're signed out and taken to the sign-in page.

If a photo couldn't be removed, your account is still deleted, and a message says how many photos are still stored. Ask a church admin to have them removed.

## What goes

- Your profile, photo and sign-in.
- Your group and team memberships, RSVPs and serving turns.
- Your prayer requests and comments, including anonymous ones and comments posted with your name hidden. Comments other people left on your requests go with them.
- Your household, when nobody else in it has an account, along with the people listed in it without an account.

If you were the only person serving on a future date, that date opens up again and the team's team leads get an email saying so.

## What stays

- Announcements, events and lessons you added, without your name as the author.
- Serving dates you shared with someone else. They stay with that person.
- Feedback you already sent, without your name.
- Your household, when someone else in it has an account, with everyone else's details in it.
- Funds that go to you. If the fund names a second person who's still in the group, it now goes to them; otherwise nobody receives it. Either way it stays off Give until a leader of the group checks it and confirms it. See [Give](/docs/use/giving#confirm-a-fund-after-its-recipient-leaves).
- Groups you were the only leader of. They have no leader until a church admin adds one.

## Delete someone else's account

A church admin can delete another person's account, including someone still waiting for approval, with **Delete account** in the members list. The confirmation shows the same **What goes** and **What stays** lists, with funds and groups counted rather than named. See [Members and households](/docs/administer/members-and-households).
