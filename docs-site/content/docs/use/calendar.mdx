## Open the calendar

Select **Calendar** in the menu. Inside a group, it shows that group's events. From **All groups**, when you're in more than one group, it shows every group's events, each in the group's color.

Choose a view with **Month**, **Week** or **List**. On a phone the calendar opens in **List**, because a month is too narrow to read there; you can still switch. In **Month** and **Week**, use the arrows to move to the previous or next month or week, and **Today** to come back. **List** shows upcoming dates; select **Show more dates** to see further ahead.

### Show some groups only

On the calendar for all your groups, the **Showing** row has a button for each group. Select a group to hide its events, and again to show them. **Show all** brings every group back.

## Open an event

Select an event to open its page. It shows:

- **Date**, **Time** and **Where**, and a pill such as **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **In 3 days** when the date is within a week.
- **About this event**, the leader's description.
- A map when the event has a location.
- **Are you going?**, when the event asks for replies.
- **Join online**, when the event has an online meeting: the meeting ID and passcode, and a button to join. Until the meeting opens, at the time the leader chose before the start, it says when one-tap join opens. While the event runs it shows **Live now**.
- **Who's coming**, listing the people who answered **Going** and then **Maybe**. A name shows with its suffix, such as Jr., when the person has added one to their profile.

For a repeating event, the page is for one date. Each date has its own replies.

## Reply to an event

When a leader turns on **Ask for RSVPs** for an event, you can answer **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't go**. Select another answer to change it.

- Answer on the event's page, in the **List** view, or in **This week** on [Home](/docs/use/group-home#this-week).
- An answer is for one date. Answering one date of a repeating event says nothing about its other dates.
- You can answer until the date's event ends.

## Get event reminders

two42 emails a reminder before a date to everyone who answered **Going** or **Maybe** for that date. The email is titled **Reminder:** with the event's name and date, and has a **View event** button.

Whether you get one depends on three things:

- **The event**: the leader chooses **1 day before**, **2 days before**, **1 week before** or **No reminder** under **Send a reminder**.
- **The group**: a leader can turn off event reminders for the whole group in Group settings.
- **You**: turn off **Email me event reminders** in [Settings](/docs/use/settings#choose-which-emails-you-get) to stop all of them.

An event that doesn't ask for replies has nobody to remind, so it sends no reminders.

If the email service asks two42 to slow down, the reminder waits a moment and is sent again, so nobody gets it twice. It can still fail to go out.

## Add one date to your calendar app

On an event's page, or beside an event in **List**, select **Add to calendar** and pick your calendar app. This copies the date once; later changes to the event don't follow it.

## Subscribe from your calendar app

A subscription keeps your calendar app up to date with your groups' events as they're added, moved or deleted.

1. On the calendar, select **Subscribe**.
2. Choose **All my groups**, shown when you're in more than one group, or one group.
3. In **Subscribe to**, choose **Open in Apple Calendar**, **Add to Google Calendar**, or **Copy link**. For another calendar app, copy the link and paste it where the app adds a calendar by URL.

The subscription includes:

- Events from 30 days ago to a year ahead. Each date of a repeating event is its own entry.
- The dates you're signed up to serve, named for the team.
- In a subscription to all your groups, each event's name starts with its group's name.

An event page's own **Subscribe** button subscribes to just that event, with every date of a repeating event.

Suppers aren't in this subscription. The Supper club page has its own **Calendar link** for your suppers. See [Supper club](/docs/use/supper-club).

<Callout type="warn">
You have one calendar link at a time. Subscribing again later, from the **Subscribe** menu or an event's **Subscribe** button, makes a new link, and every subscription you set up before stops updating. To follow several groups, subscribe once to **All my groups**.
</Callout>

A link that no calendar app has fetched for 400 days stops working. Treat the link like a password: anyone who has it sees your groups' events.

### What a subscription leaves out

A group can keep **Join details** (the meeting link, ID and passcode), **Notes** (the description) and **Location** out of members' calendar apps. Anything left out is replaced by a link to the event's page, where you see everything once signed in. Leaders set this for the group under **Calendar subscriptions** in Group settings, and can choose differently for one event.

## Add an event

Leaders see **Add event** on the calendar. If you lead more than one of the groups shown, it opens an **Add to** list; choose the group. In **Month**, when you lead exactly one of the groups shown, selecting a date opens **Add event** on that date.

Fill in the event and select **Save event**. The fields, online meetings, reminders and calendar sharing are described in [Events](/docs/administer/events).

### Repeating events

Turn on **Repeats**, then choose **How often** (**Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**) and when it **Ends**: **Never**, **After a number of times**, or **On a date**.

To change a repeating event, open the date and select **Edit event**. Under **Repeating event**, choose **This date only** or **Every date**, make the change, and select **Save changes**. **Delete event** asks the same: **This date only**, or **All dates** followed by **Delete all dates**. Members see moved and deleted dates in the next week as a notice on Home.
