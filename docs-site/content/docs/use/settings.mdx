## Open Settings

Open the account menu at the bottom of the menu, where your photo and name are, and choose **Settings**.

Settings opens once a church admin has approved you. Until then it takes you back to Home.

## Make the text larger

Under **Display**, choose a **Text size**: **Normal**, **Large** or **Larger**. The change applies straight away, on the device you're using only. Set it again on each phone, tablet or computer you use.

## Change your password

1. Under **Sign-in and security**, select **Change password**.
2. Enter your **Current password**. If you can't remember it, use the link under the field to get a reset email.
3. Enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters, then enter it again in **Type your new password again**.
4. Select **Save password**.

## Change your email

1. Under **Sign-in and security**, select **Change email**.
2. Enter your **New email address** and select **Send confirmation links**.
3. Open the confirmation link sent to your old address and the one sent to your new address.

The change happens once you've opened both links. Until then you keep signing in with your current address, shown at the top of **Sign-in and security**.

## Choose which emails you get

Under **Notifications**, turn each switch on or off. Each change saves as soon as you make it.

- **Email me event reminders**: reminders before an event date you answered Yes or Maybe to. Turning this off stops them for every event. See [Calendar](/docs/use/calendar).
- **Email me new announcements**: emails asking your serving teams to fill open dates, both the ones a team lead or leader sends with **Email the team** and the monthly email listing dates that still need someone. See [Serving](/docs/use/serving).
- **Email me about dinner club dinners**: your table once the tables are published, a dinner's new date when it moves, and the reminder the day before each dinner. Turning this off stops all three.

<Callout type="info">
Posting on the **Announcements** page doesn't send email, so **Email me new announcements** doesn't change anything there. None of these switches stops the reminder you get before a date you're serving on.
</Callout>

## Send feedback

1. Under **Feedback**, choose **An idea** or **Something's broken**.
2. Describe it in **Tell us what happened, or what you'd like**.
3. Select **Send feedback**.

Your feedback goes to your church's church admins, with your name on it.

## Download your data

Under **Your data**, select **Download my data**. Your browser saves a file named `my-two42-data.json`.

The file holds your profile, your household and anyone listed in it without their own account, your groups, RSVPs, serving turns, dinner club sign-ups with their address and food notes, dinner club replies, prayer requests, comments on prayer requests, the funds you receive, your feedback, your requests for a new group, and the message you sent with your request for access.

It doesn't include your photo, prayers you responded to, funds you're second on, requests to join a group, your teacher listing, your calendar links, or anything you added for a group, such as events and announcements.

## Delete your account

**Delete my account** is also under **Your data**. Read [Delete your account](/docs/use/delete-account) before you use it.

## Find help, privacy and terms

The links at the bottom of Settings open **Help**, which starts an email to the two42 team, and the **Privacy** and **Terms** pages.
