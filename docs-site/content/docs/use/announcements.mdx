---
title: Announcements
description: Read a group's news, and add, schedule, edit and delete announcements as a leader.
---

## Read announcements

Select **Announcements** in the menu. The page lists the group's published announcements, newest first, each with its title, the date it was published, and its content.

Recent announcements also show under **New in the group** on the group's [Home](/docs/use/group-home#new-in-the-group).

Announcements aren't emailed. Members see them in two42 only.

## Add an announcement

Leaders of the group can add announcements.

1. On **Announcements**, select **Add announcement**.
2. Enter a **Title** and the **Content**.
3. Under **When to publish**, choose one:
   - **Publish now**: members see it straight away.
   - **Schedule**: enter the date and time under **Publish on**. Times are in your church's time zone. Members see it from then.
   - **Save as draft**: members don't see it until you publish it.
4. Select the button at the bottom: **Publish announcement**, **Schedule announcement** or **Save draft**.

## Find drafts and scheduled announcements

Leaders see **Drafts and scheduled** at the top of **Announcements**, with each one marked **Draft** or **Scheduled**. Select one to edit or publish it. Unpublished announcements also show in the leader's **To do** on Home.

## Edit an announcement

Select **Edit** on a published announcement, or select a draft or scheduled one. Change it, choose **When to publish** again if needed, and select the button at the bottom.

## Delete an announcement

1. Open the announcement for editing.
2. Select **Delete announcement**.
3. Confirm with **Delete announcement**.

Members no longer see it, and it can't be brought back.
