title: Home
description: What Home shows when you're in one group, in several, or in none.
---

## Open Home

Select **Home** in the menu, or the wordmark at the top. What you see depends on how many groups you're in:

- **One group**: that group's Home.
- **Two or more groups**: a Home that covers all of them. Inside a group, **Home** in the menu opens that group's own Home.
- **No group**: **You are not in a group yet**. See [Joining a group and a household](/docs/use/joining).

## A group's Home

A group's Home opens with a greeting and today's date, then shows only the parts that have something in them.

### Starting now

When an event with an online meeting is about to begin, a **Starting now** panel appears at the top with a button to join the meeting, or the meeting ID and passcode when there's no link. It appears from the time the leader chose under **Join opens before start** until the event ends, and only when the leader turned on **Show the Join button on Home** for that event.

After the event ends, if the group has lessons, the panel changes to **This event has ended** with **Watch the recording**, which opens [Lessons](/docs/use/lessons). It stays for three hours.

### Changed dates

A notice appears when a date of a repeating event in the next 7 days was deleted or moved: for example **No** event **on** date, with the date of the next one, or the event's name and date followed by **moves**, with where and when it moved to.

### To do

Leaders see a **To do** list for the group:

- People who asked to join, with **Approve or decline**, which opens the requests in Group settings.
- Teams with open dates still to fill, and set-date teams whose last date is within 14 days.
- Announcements saved as drafts or scheduled but not yet published.

A team lead who isn't a leader sees only their own set-date teams that are running out of dates.

### Serving

If you're on a team with open dates and you aren't a leader, a card says the team **needs someone** or **needs people**, with the number of open dates and the next one. Select it to open the team's dates. See [Serving](/docs/use/serving).

### This week

**This week** lists the group's events in the next 7 days, in date order. For an event that asks for replies, you can answer **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't go** right in the row. Events that repeat daily or weekly don't ask here; answer on the event's page. Dates you're serving on are marked **You're serving**.

Birthdays and anniversaries of group members this week show at the end. Select **Calendar** beside the heading to open the full calendar.

### New in the group

The three newest things from the last 14 days: lessons, announcements, answered prayer requests, and people who joined the group. People hidden from the directory aren't listed as joining.

### Prayer

One open prayer request from the group, with **All requests** to open [Prayer](/docs/use/prayer).

### An empty group

When a group has nothing yet, Home shows **No upcoming events.**, **No announcements yet.** and **No prayer requests yet.** Leaders get **Add event** and **Add announcement** buttons there.

## Home for several groups

With two or more groups, Home shows:

- **Your groups**: a card for each group with its next event, and its number of announcements and open prayer requests. Select a card to open the group.
- **This week**: the events in the next 7 days across all your groups. If there are none, it says **Nothing on the calendar this week.**
- **Find a group**, beside **Your groups**, when your church lets members find groups.
