## Sign in

1. Select **Sign in** at the top of any page.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

You land on **Home**. If you opened a page that needs you to be signed in, you go back to that page instead.

If the email and password don't match, the page says so. Try again, or reset your password.

## Get an account

There is no open sign-up form. You get an account through one of these:

- **A group invite link** from a leader. You enter your name and email, and two42 emails you a link to set up your account. See [Join with an invite link](/docs/use/joining#join-with-an-invite-link).
- **A household invite link**. You ask for an account, and a church admin emails you the setup link once they approve it. See [Join your household](/docs/use/joining#join-your-household).
- **An invite from a church admin**, who sends the setup link to your email directly.

The sign-in page repeats this under **No account yet? Use an invite link**.

## Set up your account

The setup email links to **Set up your account**.

1. Check the **Email** shown. It's the address the invite was sent to and can't be changed here.
2. Enter a **Password** of at least 8 characters, then enter it again in **Confirm password**.
3. Select **Create account**.

You're signed in straight away. If you came from a group invite, you go to that group's Home; otherwise you go to Home.

A setup link from a group invite or an approved request works for 7 days. A link from an invite a church admin sent you works for 14 days. Each link works once.

If the page says **This link isn't valid anymore**, the link has expired or been used. If you already set up your account, sign in. Otherwise ask your church admin for a new link. If it says **Your account is already set up**, select **Sign in**.

## Set up your profile

The first time you sign in, two42 asks you to set up your profile before anything else. **Set up your profile** has four steps: **About you**, **Contact**, **Household** and **Work**. Select **Continue** to move on, **Back** to go back, and **Finish setup** at the end.

The **Household** step is where you find or add your household. See [Join your household](/docs/use/joining#join-your-household). Everything else on these steps is covered in [Profile](/docs/use/profile).

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your **Email** and select **Send link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link. It can take a few minutes to arrive, and may land in spam. Select **Send again** if it doesn't come.
4. On **Choose a new password**, enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters, enter it again in **Confirm password**, and select **Save password**.

You're signed in and taken to Home.

The page says a link is on its way whether or not an account uses that email, so it never tells anyone which addresses have accounts.

To change your password while signed in, use [Settings](/docs/use/settings#change-your-password).

## Wait for approval

If your church hasn't approved your account, or a church admin has moved you back to pending, Home shows **Waiting for approval**. You can still open **Help**, **Privacy** and **Terms**, and **Sign out**. Once a church admin approves you, your groups appear on Home. A person moved back to pending gets the same groups and teams back when they're approved again.

## Sign out

Open the account menu at the bottom of the menu, where your photo and name are, and select **Sign out**.
