## Open the directory

Select **Directory** in the menu. It has five tabs:

- **Members**: people, listed by last name.
- **Households**: the people who live together, listed together.
- **Birthdays** and **Anniversaries**.
- **Groups**: your groups and their members, and groups you can join.

Select a person or household to see their details beside the list, or on a phone in place of it.

## Choose whose people you see

Above the people tabs, **Show people from** chooses the list:

- **This group**, inside a group, or one of your other groups by name.
- **All my groups**: everyone in any of your groups.
- **Everyone in** your church, when a church admin has turned on **Directory visible to the whole church**.

Without that church setting, you only ever see people who share a group with you. A household shows when anyone in it with an account is in the list you chose. The choice doesn't show when there's only one option.

Use **Search by name** to narrow the **Members** or **Households** list. When one group is chosen and it has teams or labels, a filter button on **Members** narrows the list to people on chosen **Teams** or with chosen **Labels**.

## What a person's entry shows

A person's entry shows whatever they've added to their profile:

- Their photo, name, household, and relationship in the household.
- The teams they serve on.
- Mobile, home and work phones, with a **Text** link beside the mobile.
- Email, unless they've hidden it.
- Address: their own, or their household's.
- Birthday, as a month and day.
- Occupation and employer.

Select **Save to my phone contacts** to download them as a contact card. On your own entry, **Edit my details** opens your [profile](/docs/use/profile).

People only show what they choose to add. There's no switch to hide a phone number or address; they leave it out of their profile instead. A person with no address of their own shows their household's, so to keep an address out of the directory, leave it off the household too or hide yourself from the directory.

## Households

A household's entry lists its members with an account, then others in the household such as children, with the household's home phone, address and anniversary. On your own household's entry, **Edit household** opens it on your profile.

How to change your household is in [Profile](/docs/use/profile#your-household). How to join one is in [Joining a group and a household](/docs/use/joining#join-your-household).

## Birthdays and anniversaries

**Birthdays** and **Anniversaries** list the dates for the people you can see, using the same **Show people from** choice. Choose **This week**, **This month** or **All**. An anniversary shows for a household with a spouse.

## Groups

The **Groups** tab lists your groups first, marked **You lead this group** or **You are a member**, with the number of members. Select one to see who's in it.

When your church lets members find groups, the tab also lists other groups with **Join** or **Request to join**, the same as [Find a group](/docs/use/joining#find-a-group).

With their group chosen on **Members**, leaders also see **Edit members**, **Add member**, and **Label members** when the group has labels. See [Members and households](/docs/administer/members-and-households).

## Hide yourself from the directory

You have two switches on [your profile](/docs/use/profile#hide-details-from-the-directory). Select **Save changes** after changing either.

### Hide my email from the directory

Your email no longer shows on your directory entry, to anyone. Church admins still see it on their own pages, and two42 still uses it to sign you in and send your emails.

### Hide me from the directory

You leave the directory altogether: the people tabs, households, birthdays and anniversaries, and group member lists in the directory. You also aren't listed as joining a group on Home.

When everyone in a household who has an account and isn't pending is hidden, the household and everyone in it leave the directory too, including children and others without an account.

Hiding yourself doesn't take you out of your groups. You still see the directory and everything else in your groups.

## Who isn't in the directory

- People hidden from the directory, as above.
- People waiting for approval, or moved back to pending by a church admin.
