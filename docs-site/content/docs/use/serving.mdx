## How serving works

A **team** is a set of members in a group who take turns serving. Each team has a schedule of Sundays, and each Sunday takes one sign-up: you alone, or you and your spouse. Leaders and team leads set up teams as described in [Teams](/docs/administer/teams).

**Serving** appears in the menu once you're on a team whose team lead or leader has turned on **People can sign up**.

## Open the Serving page

Select **Serving** in the menu. The page has two lists:

- **Sign up to serve**: teams taking sign-ups. Each shows how many dates are covered and the next open one, such as **3 of 8 dates covered · next open** and a date, and who is on the team. **You're on this team** marks your own teams.
- **Teams**: teams that list their members but don't take sign-ups.

Select a team to open its dates.

## Take a date

1. Open your team.
2. Find an open Sunday and select **Sign up**.
3. If two42 asks, answer:
   - **Who's serving?**: **Me &** your spouse, or **Just me**. This shows when your spouse is on the same team.
   - **Also serve** the following weeks?: when the team asks for more than one week at a time. Choose to take the next open weeks too (for example **Yes, both weeks**), or just the date you picked.

   When both questions show, answer each and select **Sign up**.

The date then shows your name. If someone took the date at the same moment, two42 tells you and the date stays theirs.

Only people on the team can sign up, along with the group's leaders. Anyone else in the group sees the dates as **Open** and the message **Only people on this team can sign up. Ask a leader or the team lead to add you.**

## Change or cancel a date

On a date you signed up for:

- Without a spouse on the team, select **Cancel**, then **Cancel serving** to confirm, or **Keep serving** to leave it.
- With a spouse on the team, select **Change**. Choose who's serving and select **Save**, or select **Cancel this date**.

Cancelling opens the date for someone else, and the team's leads get an email that it's open again. Team leads and leaders can also remove anyone from a date.

## See past dates

Below the schedule, the team page lists past Sundays from the team's first sign-up on, with who served or **No one signed up**.

## Serving emails

| Email | When | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **You're signed up** | Right after you sign up | You. A calendar file is attached so you can add the date to your calendar. |
| **Reminder: you're serving with** the team **on** the date | On the days and at the time the team chose, before the next Sunday | Everyone signed up for that Sunday |
| The team's name, then **dates that still need someone** | On the 1st of each month, when the team has open dates; also whenever a team lead or leader uses **Email the team** | Team members who have **Email me new announcements** on in [Settings](/docs/use/settings#choose-which-emails-you-get) |
| The team's name, the date, then **is open again** | When someone cancels | The team's leads |

The sign-up and reminder emails have a **Cancel my turn** button. The open-dates email has a **Sign up** button beside each date, and **View schedule** for the rest.

Depending on the group's setting, these buttons either act straight away or ask you to sign in first. When they ask, the page says **This group asks people to sign in before responding to a serving email.**

You can't turn off the reminder before a date you're serving on; cancel the date instead.

## Run a team

Team leads and leaders see more on a team's page:

- **Team settings**: turn **People can sign up** on or off, choose the **Serving dates** (**Rolling**, the next number of weeks, or **Set dates** from one date to another), choose **Ask for more weeks?**, and choose the **Reminder email days** and **Reminder email time**. The time is in the church's time zone.
- **Email the team**: send the team the dates that are still open.
- The team roster: add and remove members.

When signing up is off, the page says **Signing up is off for this team. Turn it on in Team settings.** Details are in [Teams](/docs/administer/teams).
