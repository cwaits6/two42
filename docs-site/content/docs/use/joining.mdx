title: Joining a group and a household
description: Join a group from an invite link or Find a group, ask for a new group, and join your household.
---

## Ways into a group

- **An invite link** from a leader of the group.
- **Find a group**, when your church lets members find groups.
- **A leader or church admin adds you** to the group.
- **Your church has only one group.** A new account joins it automatically, unless it was made from a household invite link. That account waits for approval and is not added to the group; a leader or church admin adds it.

Until you're in a group, Home says **You are not in a group yet** and offers what your church allows: **Find a group**, **Add a group** or **Request a group**.

## Join with an invite link

Invite links look like an address ending in `/join/` and a long code. What happens depends on whether you have an account.

### If you don't have an account yet

1. Open the link. The page names the group and your church.
2. Enter your **Name** and **Email**, and select **Continue**.
3. Open the email two42 sends you and follow its link to set up your account. See [Set up your account](/docs/use/sign-in#set-up-your-account).

You join the group when your account is created, with no separate approval: the leader who made the link vouches for you. If the email doesn't arrive, check spam or select **Send again**.

If you already have an account, the page tells you to sign in and open the link again. Select **Sign in** under **Already have an account?**

### If you already have an account

1. Open the link and sign in if asked.
2. Select **Join group**.

What happens next depends on the group:

- **A private group**, or a group set to **Open**: you join at once and go to the group's Home.
- **A group set to Approval required**: the page says **Request sent. A leader of** the group **will review it.** You join once a leader approves.

If you're already in the group, the page says so and offers **Open group**. If you're signed in with an account from another church, or one still waiting for approval, the page says **This link is for a different account**; sign out and open the link again.

### When a link stops working

A leader can set a link to expire after a time or a number of people, and can revoke it. A link that no longer works shows **This link isn't valid anymore. Ask a leader of the group for a new one.** Leaders make and manage links as described in [Invite links](/docs/administer/invite-links).

## Find a group

**Find a group** is in the menu only when a church admin has turned on **Members can find and request to join groups**. It lists the groups in your church you can see, with the number of members and the meeting time and place when the group has one.

Each group shows one of these:

- **Join**: the group is set to **Open**. Select it to join at once.
- **Request to join**: the group is set to **Approval required**. Select it to send a request; the row then shows **Request sent** until a leader approves or declines it.
- **You are a member**, with **Open group**, or **You lead this group**.

Private groups don't appear here. Church admins see them marked **Private · invite only**; members join a private group only by invite link or when a leader adds them.

The same list, with the same buttons, is on the **Groups** tab of the [Directory](/docs/use/directory).

## Add or ask for a new group

Depending on your church's setting, **Find a group**, the **Groups** tab of the directory, and the no-group Home show one of these:

- **Add a group**: when the church lets any member add a group. Enter a **Group name** and, if you like, the **Meeting time and place**, then select **Add group**. You become the group's leader.
- **Request a group**: when a church admin approves new groups. Enter the same details and select **Send request**. Your request shows as **Waiting for approval** until a church admin reviews it.

When only church admins add groups, members see neither button; church admins always see **Add a group**. How church admins set this is in [Groups](/docs/administer/groups).

## Join your household

A household is the people who live together and are listed together in the directory.

### While setting up your profile

On the **Household** step of **Set up your profile**:

1. Select **Find my household**. two42 looks for a household under your last name.
2. If one is yours, select **Join this household**. Otherwise select **Add a new household**, or **Add household** when none was found.

When you add a household, you can add the people you live with on the same step. See [Profile](/docs/use/profile#your-household) for managing it later.

### From a household invite link

Household invite links look like an address ending in `/join/family/` and a long code. The page shows who you were **Invited as** and the household.

- **If you don't have an account**: enter your **Name**, add a **Message (optional)**, and select **Request access**. The email is the one the invite was sent to. A church admin reviews the request and emails you a link to set up your account.
- **If you have an account with the invited email**: select **Join household**.

If you're signed in with a different email, the page says **This link is for a different account**. If you're already in another household, the page says **You're already in a household**: only a church admin can move you between households.

### From a church admin's invite

When a church admin invites you to the church and names your household, setting up your account puts you in that household. You don't need to do anything else.
