title: Lessons
description: Watch your group's lesson recordings and read their summaries, and, as a leader, add lessons and series.
---

## Find a lesson

Choose **Lessons** in the menu. Every member of the group can see its lessons.

At the top, **Most recent** shows the latest lesson in a current series, or the latest lesson that isn't in a series when there is none. Select **View lesson** to open it.

Below it, pick how to list the rest:

- **By series**: each current series with its lessons, then **Other lessons** for lessons that aren't in a series, then **Past series**.
- **All lessons**: every lesson, newest first.

Lessons in a series are numbered **Part 1**, **Part 2** and so on by date, oldest first.

## Watch a lesson

Open a lesson to see its recording, its date, its series and teacher, and its **Lesson summary**.

- A YouTube recording plays on the page. **Watch on YouTube** opens it on YouTube.
- Any other recording opens in a new tab when you select **Watch recording**.

When the lesson is part of a series, **More from** the series lists other lessons in it. Select **View series** to see the whole series, with its teacher, its number of lessons and the months it ran.

## Add a lesson

Leaders of the group can add, edit and delete lessons and series. Other members see the lessons only.

1. On **Lessons**, select **Add lesson**.
2. Under **Series**, pick a series, or leave **No series**. To start a new one, select **Add series**, enter a **Series name** and, if you like, a **Teacher**, then select **Add series**.
3. Enter a **Title** and a **Recording link**. The link must start with `http://` or `https://`.
4. Optionally add a **Date**, a **Description** of one or two sentences, and a **Lesson summary** for anyone who missed it.
5. Select **Save lesson**.

To add a lesson straight into a series, select **Add lesson** on that series' page.

## Find lessons that need a summary

As a leader, choose **Needs summary** on the Lessons page. It lists every lesson without a lesson summary and shows how many there are. Select **Edit** beside a lesson to add one.

## Edit or delete a lesson

1. Open the lesson and select **Edit lesson**.
2. Change what you need and select **Save lesson**.

To delete it, select **Delete lesson** on the same page, then **Delete lesson** again to confirm. Its recording link and lesson summary go with it, and this can't be undone.

## Manage series

Select **Manage series** on the Lessons page to see every series with its teacher, number of lessons, and whether it's current or past.

- **Add series**: enter a **Series name** and an optional **Teacher**, then select **Add series**.
- **Edit**: change the name or teacher. Turn on **Past series** when the series has finished; it then shows under **Past series** on the Lessons page. Select **Save series**.
- **Delete series**: on the series' edit page, select **Delete series** and confirm. Its lessons stay, but no longer belong to a series.
