## Where requests wait

Church admins review every kind of request on **Requests** in the church admin pages. The page list shows the number waiting beside **Requests**, and the page lists only the kinds with something waiting:

| Section | What it is | Who else can review it |
|---|---|---|
| **Access requests** | People asking to join the church | Nobody |
| **Group requests** | Members asking to start a group | Nobody |
| **Join requests** | People asking to join a group, listed under each group's name | The group's leaders, in **Group settings** → **Requests** |

When nothing is waiting, the page says "No requests waiting."

## Approve an access request

An access request comes from someone with a household invite who has no account yet. They open the invite link, enter their name and an optional message, and choose **Request access**. Each request shows their name, email, message and the date they asked.

- Choose **Approve** to send them an email with a link to set up their account. The link works for 7 days. When they set it up, they are a member of the church and listed in the household that invited them.
- Choose **Decline**, then **Decline request** to confirm. They get no invite and can ask again later.

If the email can't be sent, the approval is undone and the request stays waiting, so you can try again.

People who join through a group's [invite link](/docs/administer/invite-links), or whom a church admin invites from **Members**, don't need approval: they never appear here.

## Approve a request for a new group

When **Who can add groups** in Church settings is **Approval required**, a member who chooses **Request a group** files a group request with a name and an optional meeting time and place. Each request shows the group name, who asked, and when. A name shows with its suffix, such as Jr., when the person has added one to their profile.

- Choose **Approve** to add the group. The person who asked becomes its leader.
- Choose **Decline** to turn it down.

See [Groups](/docs/administer/groups#add-a-group).

## Approve a request to join a group

A member asks to join a group when they find it and its **How people join** setting is **Approval required**. A member who follows the group's invite link while the group isn't private and needs approval also files a request.

Leaders review their group's requests in **Group settings** → **Requests**. Church admins can review any group's requests on **Requests**.

- Choose **Approve** to add them to the group.
- Choose **Decline** to turn it down. On the church admin **Requests** page you confirm with **Decline request**. They can ask again later.

## Approve a pending person

A pending person has signed in but has no access to the church yet, or was moved back to pending by a church admin. Pending people are listed on **Members** under the **Pending** tab, and don't show on **Requests**.

1. Open **Members** and choose the **Pending** tab.
2. Choose the person's name to open **Edit member**.
3. Under **Role**, choose **Member** or **Church admin**.
4. Confirm. For a member, the button reads **Approve member**.

They get access to the church and to any groups and teams they were already in.

## Move someone back to pending

Moving a person back to pending takes away their access without deleting them.

1. Open **Members**.
2. Open the menu at the end of the person's row and choose **Move to pending**.
3. Confirm with **Move to pending**.

They lose access to their groups and teams. Both come back, with any leader and team lead roles, when they are approved again. You can't move yourself to pending.
