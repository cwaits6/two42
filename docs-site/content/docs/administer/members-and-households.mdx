title: Members and households
description: How a church admin invites, edits and deletes people, and keeps the church's households current.
---

## Find a person

**Members** lists everyone in the church with their name, email, role and status. Only church admins open it.

- Search by name or email with **Search members**.
- Filter by **Role**: **All roles**, **Member** or **Church admin**.
- Choose a tab: **All**, **Invited** or **Pending**. Each shows how many people it holds.

**Invited** lists people a church admin invited, or approved, who haven't set up their account yet. **Pending** lists people waiting for approval. See [Approve people](/docs/administer/approving-people#approve-a-pending-person).

Choose a person's name, or anywhere on their row, to open **Edit member**. The menu at the end of each row offers **Edit**, **Move to pending** and **Delete account**. An invited person's row has no menu, because there is no account to act on yet.

## Invite a person

1. On **Members**, choose **Invite member**.
2. Enter their **Email**, **First name** and **Last name**.
3. If they live in a household that's already set up, choose it under **Household (optional)**. They are listed in that household and joined to it when they set up their account.
4. Choose **Send invite**.

They get an email with a link to set up their account. The link works for 14 days. When they set it up, they are a member of the church.

An address that already has an account, an invite or a request to join can't be invited again: the form says no invite was sent. To invite many people at once, see [Imports](/docs/administer/imports).

## Edit a person

**Edit member** shows the person's role and their profile.

- **Role**: choose **Member** or **Church admin**, then confirm. Making someone a church admin lets them manage the church's people, households, groups and settings. Changing a church admin to member takes away the church admin pages.
- The profile form holds their name, email, phone numbers, address, birthday and the rest of what shows in the directory, and **Save changes** saves it. Only a church admin can change a person's **Email** here; members change their own in Settings.
- **Hide email from the directory** and **Hide from the directory** can be set for them here too. When everyone in a household who has an account and isn't pending is hidden, the household leaves the directory as well.
- **Household** puts them in a household, or **No household**. **Manage households** opens the Households page.

You can't change your own role. A church needs at least one church admin, so the last one can't be changed to member; add another church admin first.

## Delete a person's account

1. On **Members**, open the menu at the end of the person's row and choose **Delete account**.
2. Read what goes and what stays, then choose **Delete account**.

This permanently deletes them from the church and can't be undone. Their profile, photo, sign-in, group and team memberships, RSVPs, serving turns, prayer requests and comments go. The confirmation also lists what stays: announcements, events and lessons they added stay without their name, and any household that others still live in stays. It also says how many groups will be left with no leader, and how many funds that go to them will be off Give until a leader confirms them or chooses who receives them. A household nobody else lives in is deleted with them.

You can't delete your own account here; members delete their own from Settings. The last church admin's account can't be deleted, and neither can an operator's.

## Households

A household is the people who live together and are listed together in the directory. They share an address and home phone there. **Households** lists every household with its address and members. Search with **Search households** by household, member or address.

### Add a household

1. Choose **Add household**.
2. Enter a **Household name**.
3. Add the address, **Home phone** and **Anniversary** if you have them. The anniversary shows on the household's directory card only when a spouse is listed.
4. Choose **Add household**.

To put a person with an account in a household, open them in **Members** and choose the household under **Household**.

### Edit a household

Choose **Edit** beside the household. Editing adds two things that aren't there when you add one:

- **Household photo**: choose **Add photo**, **Replace photo**, or remove it.
- **Listed without an account**: people in the household who don't have their own account, such as children. Choose **Add someone** and enter a first name, last name, **Relationship** (**Child**, **Spouse**, **Parent**, **Sibling** or **Other**) and an optional birthday. Everything you add shows in the directory, except a birth year. You don't have to list a child, and a child listed without an account can't be invited.

Choose **Save changes** when you're done. To take someone listed without an account out of the household, choose the remove button beside them, then **Remove from household**.

### Delete a household

Choose **Edit** beside the household, then **Delete household**, and confirm with **Delete household**. Its members with an account are left without a household. People listed in it without an account are deleted. This can't be undone.

A household's primary person and spouse can also edit their own household from their profile. See [Directory](/docs/use/directory).
