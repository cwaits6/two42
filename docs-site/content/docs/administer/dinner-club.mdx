---
title: Dinner club
description: "Run a season of dinners in members' homes: sign-ups, the shuffle into tables, dates, and who sees an address."
---

A dinner club is a group's dinners in members' homes, run as **seasons**. Members sign up as a **party** of one or two, a leader shuffles the parties into **tables**, and each table has a **host** whose home it meets at for each **dinner**. A church that wants a dinner club for everyone makes a group for it and uses the same page.

## Turn it on

In **Group settings › Details**, turn on **Dinner club** and save. The group's menu gains **Dinner club**, after Serving. It starts off for every group. Turning it off hides the page and keeps every season, so turning it on again brings them back.

## Add a season

On the Dinner club page, choose **Add season** and fill in:

- **Title**: your own name for it, such as "Supper Club".
- **Format**, one per season:
  - **Same table, one host**: the tables stay the same all season, and each table's host party hosts every dinner.
  - **Same table, hosts take turns**: the tables stay the same, and the parties at each table who can host take turns.
  - **New tables each dinner**: a new shuffle for every dinner, from the hosts free that night. Nobody hosts two dinners in a row unless you allow it.
- **Start date** and **Length**, as a number of months or an end date.
- **How often**: every few weeks on a day, or monthly on a weekday such as the 2nd Saturday, and a **Start time**.
- **Table size**: the fewest and most people at a table, 6 to 8 to start.
- **Sign-ups close**: the last day members can sign up or change their sign-up.

The dinner dates are proposed from these. On the season's page you can **Add date**, **Move date** and **Delete date**, before and after publishing, and **Propose dates again** before publishing. A season starts as a draft that members don't see; **Delete season** removes a draft.

Only one season per group takes sign-ups or runs at a time.

## Sign-ups

Choose **Open sign-ups**. Members then see the season on the Dinner club page and sign up alone, with someone in their household, or with anyone else in the group. A person is in one party per season. A party who can host gives their seats (their own party included) and the address for the dinners, which starts from their household's directory address. Depending on the format they can say which date they'd like to host or which dates they can't. Anyone can add their allergies and food needs. Kids aren't seated: a party is the adults coming.

The **Sign-ups** list on the season's page shows each party, the number of parties, hosts, people and seats, and a warning when there are too few hosts: people divided by the most per table, rounded up, for each dinner when tables change each dinner, and for the season otherwise. **Add party** signs someone up, with one other person if they're coming together, and **Remove party** takes a party off while sign-ups are open.

## Shuffle and publish

On the season's page choose **Make tables**, then **Shuffle**. The shuffle keeps each party together, gives every table a host, keeps each table within its host's seats (the fewest seats of its hosts when they take turns), evens out the table sizes, avoids leaving one single person alone among couples, and keeps apart people who already ate together this season. The draft is only for leaders. Change it with **Shuffle again**, **Move or swap** on a party, or **Host** on a table. Warnings show a table over its seats, a dinner with no host, a repeat pairing and a host with two dinners in a row.

**Publish tables** shows each member their own table and emails each person their dinners, dates and host. After publishing you can change the tables and choose **Publish changes**; only the people whose table or host changed get an email. Emails count toward the church's daily email limit; if it is reached, the tables are still published and no email goes out.

## Dinners

Each table's dinner takes its date's time. Its host can **Move dinner** to another date and time within the season, and a leader can move any table's dinner. Moving a dinner emails the people at that table, except the person who moved it, and clears its replies so people answer again. Moving a season date moves each table's dinner on it, except one its host already moved.

Each person answers **Going** or **Not going** for each of their dinners, and can answer for their partner. The host sees who is going, the counts, and the food needs of the parties coming. Replies stop once the dinner starts.

The day before each dinner, everyone at the table who hasn't said Not going gets a reminder with the time, host, address and who's coming. A person who turns off **Email me about dinner club dinners** in Settings gets no dinner club email.

## Who sees what

- **A host's address**: the host, the group's leaders, and the people seated at that host's table, only after tables are published.
- **Allergies and food needs**: the person, their partner, the group's leaders, and the host of a dinner they're at.
- **Tables**: each member sees only their own. Leaders see every table.

A church admin who isn't in the group sees none of it.

Dinners never appear on the group calendar, on Home, or in the group's calendar subscription. Each person has their own **Calendar link** on the Dinner club page for their own dinners, with times and addresses. **Reset link** ends the old one, for example if it was shared.
