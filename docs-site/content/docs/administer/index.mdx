## Who this section is for

Two roles manage two42 inside a church:

- A **church admin** manages the church's whole space: its people, households, groups and settings.
- A **leader** runs one group: its events, roster, teams, invite links and requests to join.

The two are separate. A church admin is not a member of any group unless they join it, and a leader of one group has no say over another group or over the church. One person can be both. [Roles](/docs/administer/roles) lists exactly what each can do.

## Find the pages

- **Church admin** pages open from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone, at the bottom of the **Menu** drawer). Choose **Church admin**. The item shows only for church admins. The pages are **Members**, **Households**, **Groups**, **Requests**, **Usage** and **Church settings**, led by **Back to Home**.
- **Group settings** is the last row of a group's page list, below a rule. It shows only for that group's leaders, with the number of requests to join waiting. Its pages are **Details**, **Requests**, **Privacy and joining**, **Invite links**, **Teams** and **Labels**, led by a way back to the group.

Members and leaders use the rest of the app the same way. See [Use two42](/docs/use).
