## What an invite link does

An invite link lets whoever has it join one group. Share it by text or email. It works for two kinds of people:

- **Someone new to the church.** They open the link, enter their name and email, and choose **Continue**. They get an email with a link to set up their account, which works for 7 days. When they set it up, they are a member of the church and of the group. No church admin approves them first: the link is the approval.
- **A member of the church who is signed in.** They open the link and choose **Join group**. If the group is private, or its **How people join** is **Open**, they join at once. If the group needs approval, they file a request to join, which a leader reviews in **Group settings** → **Requests**.

A link from a church that isn't theirs doesn't work for a signed-in member. An email address that a church admin declined before can't sign up through a link, and neither can one that already has an account; someone with an account signs in and opens the link again.

<Callout type="warn">
Anyone with an active link can join the group, and someone new joins the church with it. Give a link an expiry or a use limit when you share it widely, and revoke it when you're done.
</Callout>

## Add a link

1. In **Group settings**, open **Invite links**.
2. Choose **Add link**.
3. Choose when it **Expires**: **Never**, **7 days** or **30 days**.
4. Choose how many **Uses**: **No limit**, **1 person** or **10 people**.
5. Choose **Add link**.

The link shows under **Active links**. Choose **Copy link** to copy it.

Each link shows when it expires, how many times it was used, and which leader created it. A person who opens the link again counts as one use, not two.

## Revoke a link

1. Choose **Revoke link** on the link.
2. Confirm with **Revoke link**.

Anyone who has it can no longer use it. A revoked link can't be turned back on; add a new one instead.

Links that were revoked, have expired or are used up move to **Past links**, labeled **Revoked**, **Expired** or **Used up**. Anyone who opens one sees "This link isn't valid anymore" and is asked to get a new one from a leader of the group.

## Who can manage links

Leaders of the group manage its links on **Invite links**. A church admin manages them only for a group they lead.

Household invites are different: they add someone to a household, not a group. See [Join a group or household](/docs/use/joining).
