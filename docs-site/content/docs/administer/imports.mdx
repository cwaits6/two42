## Invite people from a CSV file

**Import members** sends an invite to each new address in a CSV file. Only church admins open it.

1. On **Members**, choose **Import members**.
2. Choose **Choose a CSV file**, or drag the file onto it. The file needs a column with "email" in its name, such as **Email**. A column with "name" in its name is shown beside each address; without one, the first column is used.
3. Check the list. It shows how many contacts were found, how many are new and how many already joined. People who already joined are marked **Already a member** and can't be selected. New people are selected to start.
4. Clear the ones you don't want to invite, or use **Select all new members**.
5. Choose **Send** with the number of invites, for example **Send 12 invites**.

Each person gets the same email as an [invite from Members](/docs/administer/members-and-households#invite-a-person): a link to set up their account that works for 14 days. When they set it up, they are a member of the church. They enter their own name then; the name in your file isn't sent with the invite.

After sending, the page says how many invites went out, how many were skipped because the person already joined or was already invited, and which addresses couldn't be sent to.

If the file can't be read, the page says why and lists the columns it found. Fix the file and choose it again.

### What the import doesn't do

- It doesn't add anyone to a group or household. Add people to a household from **Members** after they sign up, and to a group from the group's roster or with an [invite link](/docs/administer/invite-links).
- It doesn't send a second invite to an address that already has one.

## The member file, with no page yet

two42 also has a full member file: one row per person with their contact details, household, role, groups and directory settings. It has no page in the app.

- **Export.** A church admin who is signed in can download it by opening `/api/admin/members/export` on the church's two42 address. The file is named for the church and the date.
- **Import.** `/api/admin/members/import` takes the same file back. It checks every row before writing any, and with `?mode=validate` it reports what each row would change without writing anything; `?mode=apply` writes. It updates people it matches and adds people without an account to households. A name that comes locked from your church's records is left as it is, and that row's report says `Name is managed by your church's records; left as it is.` It can't create an account: a new row marked as having a sign-in is an error. Files are limited to 5 MB and 5,000 rows, and a file with an `org_id` column is refused. It is a request with no form in the app, so it is for people comfortable sending one with their signed-in session.
- **An import that stops partway.** Checking first doesn't make the write all-or-nothing. If writing fails, the response says `Import partially applied`, with the line it stopped at in `failedAtLine` and the lines already written in `appliedLines`. Keep that response; it is the only record of what was written. Delete the `appliedLines` rows from your file and import the rest. Importing the written lines again can create duplicate households. The `failedAtLine` row may be partly written: a new household it names can already exist even though the line isn't in `appliedLines`, and the response doesn't say so. Importing that row again reuses the household, because the import matches an existing household by name; if two households have that name, the row is an error and nothing is written. Check **Households** for what that row already created before importing it again.

The columns are listed in `MEMBER_CSV_COLUMNS` in [`lib/members/format.ts`](https://github.com/cwaits6/two42/blob/main/lib/members/format.ts). A file must have `first_name`, `last_name`, `email` and `has_login`.

## Copy details from your church's records

When the operator has set `RECORDS_KEY_SECRET`, **Church settings → Church records** lets a church admin connect the church's records (Rock RMS) and link people to them. Once connected, the **People** tab chooses what two42 copies into linked people's profiles, and the **Households** tab suggests households from the families in the records.

### Connect

The form asks for the **Rock RMS address** and a **REST key**. Make the key in Rock under Admin Tools, Settings, REST Keys, on a user made for two42 alone, in a security role of its own that can only view the **People**, **PhoneNumbers**, **DefinedValues**, **Groups**, **GroupMembers** and **Locations** REST controllers, with no edit rights. two42 checks the key can view all six before it saves it. two42 never writes to Rock.

A key saved before households came keeps copying people; until its role can view Groups, GroupMembers and Locations, the **Households** tab says families couldn't be read, and **Refresh now** says households weren't refreshed.

### What two42 copies

For each of **Name**, **Suffix**, **Title**, **Gender**, **Mobile phone**, **Work phone**, **Birthday**, **Anniversary**, **Photo**, **A child's grade**, **Address** and **Home phone**, choose:

- **Not from your church's records**: two42 doesn't touch it.
- **From them, locked**: members see **Managed by your church** and can't change it. Only name, suffix, title, gender and a child's grade can be locked; address and home phone are always the household's to change.
- **From them, member can change**: filled in, and a member who changes or clears it keeps their own from then on. They can go back with **Use your church's value**.

Select **Save what two42 copies**. The next refresh fills in what you turned on. While a refresh is running the save is refused, because that refresh works from the settings it started with; save again once it finishes. A detail a member can change that two42 already holds a different value for is left as the member's, with your church's value offered to them. A locked detail always takes your church's value. The preferred name comes from the nick name, only while a member hasn't set one. The sign-in email, unlisted phone numbers, the texting flag, connection status and your church's own fields are never copied. The family's address and home phone go to its household, not to each person, and only once you confirm the household on the **Households** tab. Either adult in the household can change or clear them, and a clear sticks.

Each member who gets a detail filled in sees a one-time notice on **My profile**. What they see is in [Profile](/docs/use/profile#details-from-your-churchs-records).

### Refresh

two42 refreshes every night, reading people changed in Rock since the last refresh and anyone not copied yet. **Refresh** shows when the last refresh finished and how many linked people are still to copy on the next refresh: people not yet copied, and everyone after you save a change to what two42 copies. A refresh that fails or runs out of time doesn't change that time. Select **Refresh now** to run it straight away; it says how many people it updated, and how many it couldn't copy or ran out of time for, who are tried again on the next refresh. If it runs out of time before households, it says **Households weren't all refreshed; the next refresh carries on.**

### Households

Rock groups people into families. The **Households** tab suggests putting each family's linked people in one household here. Nothing changes in any household until you confirm.

- **Suggest children**: off by default. When it's on, the children in a confirmed household's family are suggested to its primary and spouse, who add or dismiss each one on their **Household** tab. Only a child's first and last name, birthday without the year, grade and photo come over; a child's photo arrives at the refresh after they're added. Turning it off removes every suggestion not yet added. A dismissed child is never suggested again. While a refresh is running the switch isn't saved; switch it again once the refresh finishes.
- **Ready to confirm**: suggestions where nobody leaves a household they're in, including a household whose people are already together and only needs linking to its family. **Review list** shows each one, with **Include** to leave one out; **Confirm all** confirms them 100 at a time.
- **Check first**: someone leaves a household they're in. Check with them, then **Confirm household**, one at a time; it shows who moves and asks first.

Each suggestion names people and households from two42. Confirming moves exactly the people it names into the household, with the relationship it shows; a couple married in Rock become **Primary** and **Spouse**. Whoever stays behind keeps their household, its address, phone, photo and the people in it without an account. **Confirm household** refuses a suggestion that changed since the last refresh with **This changed since the last refresh. Choose Refresh now on the People tab, then try again.** **Confirm all** leaves any that changed as they are and says how many. While a refresh is running, confirming stops with **A refresh is running.** Any households already confirmed stay confirmed; try again once it finishes. A confirmed household gets the family's address and home phone at the next refresh, under what two42 copies.

Suggestions are worked out by each refresh, nightly or **Refresh now**; the tab shows the last refresh's.

### Needs review

**Needs review** lists linked people who became inactive in Rock or can't be found there any more, with **Open in Rock RMS** for an inactive person and **Unlink**. Nothing happens to them automatically.

### Disconnecting or unlinking

Unlinking a person, or disconnecting, keeps everything already copied in their profile as ordinary two42 details they can change. Only the link and further updates stop.

The design is in [`docs/integrations/church-records.md`](https://github.com/cwaits6/two42/blob/main/docs/integrations/church-records.md).
