## What a group is

A group is a recurring gathering of people within a church. Each church adds and names its own groups; two42 doesn't come with any. Every group has its own calendar, announcements, prayer requests, lessons, teams, funds and roster, and only its members see them.

Every group works the same way. What differs between groups is set by the group's leaders in [Group settings](/docs/administer/group-settings): whether it is private, how people join, its event reminders, what its calendar feed shares, and its teams.

A person can be in several groups. Their role is set per group: they can lead one group and be a member of another.

## See every group

**Groups** in the church admin pages lists every group in the church with its description, its leaders and its number of members. A group with no leader is marked **No leader**.

Choose a group's name to open it. For a group you're in, that opens the group itself. For any other group, it opens a page with the group's roster, leaders first, and the note that its calendar, lessons and other pages stay with its members. Choose a person there to open them in **Members**. A name shows with its suffix, such as Jr., when the person has added one to their profile. In the group's **Directory**, **Members** can also be narrowed to **Male** or **Female**; see [Directory](/docs/use/directory).

## Add a group

A church admin can always add a group:

1. On **Groups**, choose **Add group**.
2. Enter a **Group name** and, if you like, a **Meeting time and place**.
3. Under **Leader**, choose who leads it. It starts on you. If you choose someone else, you won't be a member of the group.
4. Choose **Add group**.

The group starts with its leader and an empty roster.

Members can add or ask for a group depending on **Who can add groups** in [Church settings](/docs/administer/church-admin#groups):

- **Open**: members see **Add a group**. The group goes live at once with them as its leader.
- **Approval required**: members see **Request a group**. A church admin approves it on **Requests**, and the person who asked becomes its leader. See [Approve people](/docs/administer/approving-people#approve-a-request-for-a-new-group).
- **Church admins only**: members see neither.

Members find these buttons on **Find a group** and in the Directory's groups list.

## Name a group's leaders

A group can have several leaders, each with the same rights. A church admin can change any group's leaders without joining it:

1. On **Groups**, choose **Manage leaders** beside the group.
2. Choose **Add leader**, search for a person, and choose **Add**. Someone already in the group becomes a leader; anyone else from the directory joins the group as a leader.
3. To swap one leader for another, choose **Replace** beside them and pick the new leader.
4. To take someone off the leaders, choose **Remove**. They stay in the group as a member.

Leaders can also make another member a leader, from the roster. See [Manage the roster](#manage-the-roster).

Nothing stops a group from losing its last leader. Such a group shows **No leader** on **Groups** until a church admin adds one.

## Manage the roster

A group's leaders keep its roster in the Directory, inside the group.

- **Add member** lists the people you can see in the directory who aren't in the group yet. Choose **Add** beside a person.
- **Edit members** puts a pencil and a remove button beside each person.
  - The pencil opens **Edit member**, where you set their **Role** in the group (**Leader** or **Member**) and their labels. This changes the group only, not their profile.
  - The remove button takes them out of the group after you confirm with **Remove from group**. They keep their profile and stay in their other groups.
- **Label members** applies or removes labels on several people at once. It shows when the group has labels. See [Add labels](/docs/administer/group-settings#add-labels).

You can't change your own role or remove yourself in **Edit members**; another leader can.

Who a leader can add depends on **Directory visible to the whole church**: when it is off, the list holds only people who share a group with them.

People also join through [invite links](/docs/administer/invite-links) and requests to join. See [Approve people](/docs/administer/approving-people#approve-a-request-to-join-a-group).

## Delete a group

There is no way to delete a group in the app.
