## Open Group settings

**Group settings** is the last row of a group's page list, below a rule. It shows only for the group's leaders, with the number of requests to join waiting, and opens on **Details**. A member who isn't a leader and opens a Group settings address lands on the group's Home.

The page list in Group settings starts with a way back to the group, named for it, then:

1. **Details**
2. **Requests**
3. **Privacy and joining**
4. **Invite links**
5. **Teams**
6. **Labels**

Changes on these pages apply to that group only. A church admin opens Group settings only for a group they lead.

## Edit the group's details

**Details** has five sections and one **Save changes** button for all of them. Leaving with unsaved changes asks you to confirm.

- **Name and description**: the **Group name** and **Description** members see on Home and in the group switcher.
- **Group color**: used for the group in the switcher, on Home and on its calendar events.
- **Calendar subscriptions**: what members' calendar apps get when they subscribe to this group's calendar. Turn off **Join details** (the meeting link, ID and passcode of online events), **Notes** (each event's description) or **Location** to leave them out. Anything turned off is replaced by a link to the event page, where signed-in members see everything. Each event can choose differently. All three start on.
- **Event reminders**: **Send event reminders** turns reminder emails on or off for every event in the group. **Default for new events** is what **Add event** starts on: **1 day before**, **2 days before** or **1 week before**. Reminders start on, at 1 day before.
- **Supper club**: adds the Supper club page to the group's menu. It starts off. Turning it off hides the page and keeps every rotation. See [Supper club](/docs/administer/supper-club).

Event reminders go to the people who answered Yes or Maybe for a date. Turning **Send event reminders** off stops them for the whole group without changing each event's own choice. See [Events](/docs/administer/events#set-a-reminder).

## Review requests to join

**Requests** lists the people asking to join the group, with their email and the date they asked. Choose **Approve** to add them to the group, or **Decline**. A church admin can review the same requests from **Requests** in the church admin pages.

When **How people join** is **Open**, people who find the group join at once, and the page says requests are approved automatically.

## Choose who can find and join the group

**Privacy and joining** has two settings:

- **Private group**. Hides the group from **Find a group** and the Directory. People join only by an [invite link](/docs/administer/invite-links) or when a leader adds them. The church's setting is the limit: a private group can only hide more, never less. When the church has turned off finding groups, the group is already hidden, and marking it private keeps it hidden if that changes.
- **How people join**, used while the group isn't private:
  - **Approval required**: a member asks to join, and a leader approves it. This is where it starts.
  - **Open**: any member who finds the group joins at once.

## Manage invite links

**Invite links** lists the group's links and lets you add and revoke them. See [Invite links](/docs/administer/invite-links).

## Manage teams

**Teams** holds the group's serving teams, their rosters and team leads, the serving email setting, and who has served. See [Teams](/docs/administer/teams).

## Add labels

Labels are ways to filter the members of a group, such as by table or by a shared interest. Labels belong to one group.

1. Open **Labels** and choose **Add label**.
2. Enter a **Label name** and, if you like, choose a **Color**.
3. Choose **Add label**.

Each label lists **Edit** and **Delete**. Deleting a label removes it from every member it was on.

To put labels on people, open the group's Directory and choose **Label members** to label several at once, or **Edit members** and the pencil beside a person to set their labels one at a time. Anyone in the group can filter the roster by label. See [Groups](/docs/administer/groups#manage-the-roster).
