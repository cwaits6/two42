## What a team is

A team is a set of members within a group who take turns serving. Teams belong to one group, and everyone on a team must be in that group. A leader manages them in **Group settings** → **Teams**.

A team that is a **Serving role** is listed on the group's **Serving** page with a roster everyone in the group can see. Once a leader or its team lead turns on **People can sign up**, its members take dates there. The **Serving** link shows in the sidebar only for people on a team that has sign-ups turned on. See [Serving](/docs/use/serving).

## Add a team

1. In **Group settings**, open **Teams** and choose **Add team**.
2. Enter a **Team name**, and if you like a **Description**, a **Color** and an **Icon**.
3. Turn on **Serving role** to list the team on the Serving page.
4. Choose **Add team**.

Use **Move up** and **Move down** to set the order teams are listed in. Choose **Edit** to change a team.

## Remove a team

1. Choose **Edit** on the team, then **Remove team**.
2. Confirm with **Remove team**. The confirmation says how many members will be taken off the team.

## Choose who is on a team

1. Choose **Members** on the team, or the team itself.
2. Choose **Add to team**, search for a person in the group, and choose **Add**.
3. To take someone off, choose the remove button beside their name.

To make someone the team's lead, choose the star beside their name. Choose it again to remove them as team lead. A team can have more than one team lead.

A team lead can add members of the group to their own team and remove members who aren't team leads, from the team's page under **Serving**. Only a leader makes or removes a team lead. See [Roles](/docs/administer/roles#team-leads).

## Set a team's serving dates and reminders

**Team settings** shows on each serving-role team in **Teams**, and on the team's page under **Serving** for its team leads and the group's leaders.

- **People can sign up**: turns sign-ups on for the team.
- **Serving dates**: dates fall on Sundays.
  - **Rolling**: the next number of weeks you enter in **Next**, from 1 to 26. A team with no settings saved shows the next 8 Sundays.
  - **Set dates**: every Sunday from **From** to **To**, which must be within a year.
- **Ask for more weeks?**: how many following weeks a sign-up offers in one go: **No**, or **1 more** to **3 more**.
- **Reminder email days**: the days of the week the team's reminder emails go out.
- **Reminder email time**: the hour they go out, from **6:00 AM** to **9:00 PM** in the church's time zone. It starts at **9:00 AM**.

Choose **Save settings**.

Reminder emails go to the people on the next serving date. Sending them is the job of the scheduled edge functions; see [Edge functions](/docs/run/edge-functions).

## Choose how serving emails work

Under **Serving emails** on **Teams**, **Let people respond from the email without signing in** decides whether the buttons in the group's serving emails work straight away. Turn it off to ask people to sign in first. Choose **Save setting**.

## See who has served

**Who has served** on **Teams** shows how often each member has served on each team people can sign up for, with a total. Choose a **Date range**: **This year**, **Last year**, **Last 6 months** or **All time**.
